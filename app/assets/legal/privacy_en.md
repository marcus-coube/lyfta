Lyfta Privacy Policy
Version 2026-11-v2

This policy explains which data Lyfta processes, why, and what your rights are.

1. Who processes your data
Lyfta provides the platform, and the gym, studio or personal trainer that invited you uses it to follow your training. Privacy contact: privacidade@lyfta.app.

2. Which data we process
- Identification: name, email and password (stored only as a hash).
- Financial: membership fees, due dates and manual payments recorded by your gym, studio or trainer, with your name and email.
- Health data (sensitive personal data): completed workouts, loads, body measurements and body composition.
- Progress photos (optional): images you upload.
- Communication: messages with your coach and workout check-ins, including pain reports.
- Device: a token to send notifications to your phone.

3. What we use it for
To prescribe, run and follow your training; to show your progress to you and your coach; to enable chat; to send workout reminders. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Legal basis and consent
Health data is processed with your explicit consent, given per purpose: (a) health data for training prescription and follow-up, required to use the app as a student; (b) progress photos, optional. You can withdraw each consent under Account > Privacy. Withdrawing photos blocks new uploads and your coach stops seeing them.

5. Who sees your data
The coaches of your gym, and you. Infrastructure providers (hosting, transactional email and notifications) process data only to run the service.

6. Security
Encrypted communication, photos in private storage with temporary links, hashed passwords and isolation between gyms.

7. How long we keep it
While your account exists. When you delete the account: health data and photos are permanently erased; completed workouts and messages are anonymized and no longer linked to you; the record of your consents is kept as proof, with no personal data. Financial records (membership fees and payments) keep your name and email after deletion, as required by law and for the regular exercise of rights (LGPD, art. 7, II and VI, and art. 16, I); only the organization that issued the invoices sees them. While a membership fee is open or overdue, the account cannot be deleted: settle with the organization first.

8. Your rights
Confirmation and access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent and information about sharing. Account deletion is under Account > Delete my account; for the rest, write to privacidade@lyfta.app.

9. Changes
When this policy changes, the version above changes and we will ask for your acceptance again.
